Flame of Ages — privacy policy

Version of 3 November 2026 · Français

In short

Flame of Ages is played without an account and without personal data. Nothing leaves your device unless you play online. No advertising, no analytics; nothing is sold or shared.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, ghosts, settings, the age you declared and a parent’s consent stay in the game’s files, on your device. You can export or erase them (Settings, then Profiles and saves). We have no access to them.

When you play online

Playing online (the daily leaderboards of Endless and the Odyssey, online co-op and duels) is a choice made in the game (Settings, then Play online). The game asks your age once; under 15, a parent’s consent is asked in the game before anything is sent. A child profile never plays online.

What is then sent to the server:

  • a pseudonym drawn by the game (for example Slinger-Amber-412), never your name;
  • a fingerprint of a code specific to your profile, which lets the server recognise you without knowing who you are;
  • the games sent to the daily leaderboards and to ranked duels, so the server can replay them and check the score: for Endless, the place, the wave, the score and the moves of the game; for the Daily Odyssey, the whole crossing (the stops cleared, the relics chosen, the moves of each stop and the score); your duel rating;
  • in co-op and duels, the moves of the game go through the server to your partner. Players talk only through ready-made messages; nothing is recorded.

What is never sent: your name, your age, an email address, a phone number, your location, your contacts, an advertising identifier.

As with any Internet connection, the server sees your device’s IP address while it answers you. The game does not keep it with your account; the host keeps it for a few days in its technical logs.

Why, and on what basis

This data is used only to run online play: ranking verified scores, computing duel ratings, connecting two players. Its legal basis is your consent, given in the game (and a parent’s, under 15). You can withdraw it at any time.

Who sees it

Other players see your pseudonym and verified scores on the daily leaderboards, and your pseudonym in co-op or duels. No one else: the publisher and its host, Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore), which keeps the data in the European Union (Ireland). The server’s functions and Supabase’s teams may process the data outside the Union, in particular in the United States and Singapore; Supabase is bound by contract to protect it (the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses).

How long

  • Daily leaderboard games (Endless and the Odyssey): 90 days.
  • Co-op and duel rooms: one hour.
  • The online account: as long as you keep it. An account with no connection for 24 months is deleted.

Your rights

Everything is done in the game, without writing to anyone (Settings, then Play online):

  • see and take away your data: “Copy my online data”;
  • erase it: “Delete my online account”;
  • withdraw your consent: “Withdraw consent”;
  • change your pseudonym: “Another pseudonym”.

For any other request: contact@aplomb-apps.fr. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or to the authority of your country.
