Privacy Policy — Letter Mason

Updated on 21 November 2026. The French text prevails.

1. In short

  • The game is played without an account, without third-party advertising and without audience measurement. It uses no advertising identifier and does not track you.
  • Aplomb has no server for this game and receives no data from your games.
  • Your games stay on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your iCloud.
  • Apart from what you choose to share or write to us, only two things can leave your device, and only to Apple: the iCloud copy, if iCloud is active for the game, and your scores and achievements to Game Center, only if you turn on the switch in the game's Settings (off by default).
  • To date, the game makes no network request for its word lists (section 6).
  • Aplomb receives personal data only if you write to it.
  • Settings › "Erase my data" erases the game's data on the device and its iCloud copy (section 12).

2. Data controller

Jérôme Lasalle, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel) trading as Aplomb, 4 impasse du Couchant, 78630 Morainvilliers, France, trade register RCS Versailles 109 075 572 (the "publisher"). Contact for any question about your data: contact@aplomb-apps.fr.

3. What stays on your device

In the game's folder, accessible to you alone:

  • the grid in progress and the archive grid in progress;
  • your submitted grids: letters placed, order of moves, score, date and time of submission;
  • your Sprints: language, draw, date, score, duration, grid;
  • your settings: appearance, sounds, vibrations, game language, notification and its time, Game Center switch, Full Edition status, last presentation of the Edition;
  • the confirmation of your purchase, if you bought the Full Edition: product, price, date and time, waiver of the right of withdrawal, version of the terms. It is sent to no one and is not copied to iCloud; a button in the shop lets you save or share it as a text file;
  • the latest time seen by the game, used to detect a clock set back;
  • if Game Center is on, scores and achievements waiting to be sent.

The widget reads, from a space shared between it and the game on your device, the letters and state of the coming days, your streak and your latest results.

All of this disappears if you delete the game, except the iCloud copy (section 4). You can also erase it without deleting the game (section 12).

4. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is allowed for the game, it automatically copies to your personal iCloud space:

  • your submitted grids (without the order of moves);
  • your Sprints and the number of Sprints started that day;
  • your appearance, sound, vibration and game language settings, and the notification time.

The grid in progress, the notification on/off setting, the Game Center switch and the purchase confirmation are not copied. This copy lets you find your history on your other devices and after a reinstall. The publisher has no access to it: Apple keeps it, in your account, under Apple's privacy policy.

The game has no switch for iCloud: you can stop this copy in your device's Settings, under the iCloud section of your Apple Account. Deleting the game does not erase this copy, which comes back if you reinstall it. To erase it, use Settings › "Erase my data" before deleting the game (section 12).

5. Game Center

Game Center is an Apple service. The game has a switch in its Settings, off by default. This setting belongs to each device and is not copied to iCloud.

When off, the game does not connect to Game Center, sends nothing to it and queues nothing.

When you turn it on, the game connects to Game Center, then sends to Apple, without asking you each time:

  • your achievements (8 in all), including those already earned according to your history;
  • from then on, the score of your official grid of the day, to that language's daily leaderboard, if it is submitted within that leaderboard's period;
  • from then on, the score of your first 3 Sprints of each day, to that language's weekly leaderboard.

A result screen says when a daily score has been sent. A replay, an archive challenge, Practice and the tutorial are never sent. If sending does not succeed straight away, a score waits on your device until its leaderboard's period ends.

When you turn it off, the queue on your device is emptied and nothing more is sent. What is already with Apple stays there: contact Apple to have it removed.

According to Apple, your nickname, avatar and scores on global leaderboards are visible to all Game Center users. These data are processed by Apple under its own policy ("Game Center & Privacy" page, dated 26 October 2026). The game only reads whether you are signed in; it neither reads nor keeps your nickname, player identifier or friends.

Legal basis. To the extent that the publisher is responsible for this sending, it rests on providing the leaderboard feature you request by turning on the switch (article 6.1.b GDPR).

6. Word lists: no network request to date

The game uses its built-in word lists and makes no network request to check or correct them. They are corrected through game updates.

A daily check for a corrections file will only be enabled when the first file is published, through a game update. This policy will be changed before that update to state what is transmitted, to whom and on what legal basis.

7. Morning notification

The notification is scheduled on your device: no server, no token. It is offered only once, after your first grid, and iOS asks for permission only if you accept. You can adjust or turn it off in the game's Settings and in iOS Settings.

8. Photos and sharing

If you save an image or video of your grid, the game asks for add-only access to Photos: it does not read your library. Sharing goes through the iOS share sheet, to the destination you choose. The image and video show the shape of the grid, without its letters, with the score, the estimated rank, the language, and the number and date of the challenge.

The game uses neither your location, nor your contacts, nor the microphone, nor the camera.

9. If you write to us (including "Report a word")

"Report a word" opens your mail app with a prepared message to contact@aplomb-apps.fr: the word, the game language, the dictionary version and the challenge or mode. You can edit it; nothing leaves until you send it.

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and your message. They are used only to correct the word lists and to reply to you. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in handling these requests (article 6.1.f GDPR); for a complaint about a purchase, performance of the contract; for a request about your rights, legal obligation. They are kept for three years after the last exchange, or five years for a complaint about a purchase, then deleted. They are neither sold nor shared; only the publisher's e-mail provider hosts them.

10. Purchase and App Store

The Full Edition is bought through the App Store: Apple processes your account and payment as a separate controller, under its own policy. The game checks the purchase on the device. The publisher receives neither your identity nor your payment details; it receives from Apple aggregate statistics (downloads, sales, countries) and, if you agreed to share them with developers, crash reports without identity.

The banner for another Aplomb game sends nothing and involves no tracking: if you touch it, that game's App Store page opens, with no tracking parameter. It never appears to anyone who has bought the Full Edition.

The purchase confirmation stays on your device: the publisher does not receive it.

11. Children

The game is for all audiences and does not ask for age. The publisher collects no data about children through the game. If a child has a Game Center account, Apple's restrictions and parental controls (Screen Time) apply. A parent may write to us to have a message sent by their child deleted.

12. Your rights

For the data the publisher holds (your messages), you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability under the GDPR, by writing to contact@aplomb-apps.fr. You will get an answer within one month.

The game's data is in your hands. Settings › "Erase my data", after confirmation, erases:

  • on this device: your games, results, Sprints, settings, your purchase confirmation and the Game Center queue;
  • the game's iCloud copy.

Before erasing, save your purchase confirmation if you want to keep it: the game invites you to, and it cannot be recreated.

This erasure has three limits:

  • your purchase stays with Apple: "Restore purchases" brings it back;
  • scores already sent to Game Center stay with Apple: contact Apple;
  • another device signed in to the same iCloud account keeps its copy and may send it back: erase on that device too.

For the App Store, also contact Apple.

You may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

13. Changes

No new data will be sent by the game without this policy being updated first. Each version is dated.
