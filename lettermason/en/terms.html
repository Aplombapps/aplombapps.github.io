7. Apple services: iCloud and Game Center

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game copies your history to your account so you find it on your other devices and after a reinstall. The publisher has no access to this copy. Settings › "Erase my data" erases it (details in the privacy policy).

Game Center. A switch in the game's Settings, off by default, controls Game Center. This setting belongs to each device and is not copied to iCloud. When off, the game does not connect to Game Center, sends nothing to it and queues nothing. If you turn it on, the game connects, sends the achievements already earned according to your history, then your subsequent scores; your score and nickname are visible to other players. If you turn it off, the queue on the device is emptied; what is already with Apple stays there. Without Game Center, the game remains whole.

These services are provided by Apple under its own terms, with no guarantee of permanent availability from the publisher. Scores are not checked by any publisher server; leaderboards and achievements have no market value and give no entitlement to any prize.
