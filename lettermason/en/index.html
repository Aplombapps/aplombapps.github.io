Letter Mason — support

FrançaisEspañol

Every day, the same 25 letters for all players of a language: build the densest grid you can. In English, French and Spanish, on iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later). No account and no third-party ads.

Contact us

contact@aplomb-apps.fr — a question, a bug, an idea. For a bug, tell us your device, its iOS version and what you were doing. We reply within 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the Full Edition?
A one-time purchase, with no subscription: the whole archive, unlimited Sprints, detailed statistics and three themes. It improves no score. The daily challenge, the streak and the last 7 days stay free.

I changed device: how do I get my purchase back?
Settings › Purchases › “Restore purchases”, with the same Apple Account. You are not charged. Family Sharing is not included.

How do I ask for a refund?
The purchase is handled by Apple: use your Apple Account's purchase history or reportaproblem.apple.com. Your rights are set out in section 4 of the terms.

A word is missing or offensive?
Settings › “Report a word” prepares an email. You can also write to us directly.

Is Game Center required?
No. It is off by default and turned on in Settings. When on, your scores and achievements go to Apple, and your nickname and scores are visible to other players.

How do I erase my data?
Settings › “Erase my data” erases the game from this device and its iCloud copy. Do it before deleting the game, and on each of your devices. Save your purchase confirmation first if you want to keep it.
