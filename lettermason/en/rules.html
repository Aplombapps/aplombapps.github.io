Game Rules — Letter Mason

Version of 21 November 2026.

1. The idea

Each day, all players of a language receive the same 25 letters. With these letters, you build the densest possible crossword grid on a 9 × 9 board. You play in English, French or Spanish; each language has its own draw. Letters carry no accent, except Ñ in Spanish.

2. A valid grid

To be submitted, a grid must follow three rules:

  • every run of 2 letters or more, across or down, is a word from the language's list;
  • all placed tiles form a single block, joined by their sides;
  • at least 2 tiles are placed.

While a rule is not met, "Submit" stays disabled and the game names the first problem. You do not have to place all your letters.

3. The score

The score of a valid grid is the sum of:

  • Tiles placed: 8 points per tile;
  • Crossings: 5 points per cell that belongs to both an across word and a down word;
  • Rarity: the points of each letter placed, from 4 to 20; the harder a letter is to place in the language, the more it earns;
  • Symmetry of the shape: 15 points for one axis, 20 for a half-turn rotation, 35 for two axes. Only the shape counts, not the letters, and it takes at least 2 rows and 2 columns;
  • Long words: 2 points per letter beyond 4, for each word, up to 20 points per grid;
  • Complete grid: 20 points if all 25 letters are placed;
  • Letters left in the rack: minus 3 points per letter.

The score never goes below zero.

4. Your rank and the best known grid

After you submit, the game shows an estimate: "above X% of today's reference grids". Your grid is compared with 5,000 grids built by our robots for this draw, not with other players' grids. This percentage therefore does not tell you your place among players.

The best known grid is the best one found by our solver and robots. It is not necessarily the best possible: it can be beaten, and the game tells you if you do.

5. The daily challenge

  • The challenge changes at midnight, your device's local time.
  • Your first submitted grid is your official grid. You can then replay it as a "replay", which counts for neither the streak nor the leaderboard.
  • The game contains 400 draws per language, up to 1 December 2027. After that, until an update adds more, the draws come back in the same order.

6. The streak and the joker

  • Your streak counts the days on which you submitted a daily challenge, in any language.
  • A grid submitted before 3 am still counts for the day that challenge was opened.
  • One joker a month automatically covers a missed day. A second missed day in the same month ends the streak. The joker is never sold.
  • Archive challenges do not count for the streak.
  • If the device's clock has been set back, a grid submitted at that time counts for neither the streak nor the leaderboard.

7. The other modes

Archive. Past challenges can be played with their best known grid and their estimate. The last 7 days are free; older ones are part of the Full Edition. Your scores always stay visible. The archive counts for neither the streak nor the leaderboards.

Sprint. 15 letters, a 7 × 7 grid, 3 minutes, with a pause that stops the clock and hides the grid. The score kept is that of your last valid grid; the complete-grid bonus is 12 points. A Sprint you have started does not resume if you close the game. 3 Sprints a day are free; the Full Edition makes them unlimited.

Practice. Free draws, with a help feature that suggests words. Nothing is saved or ranked. This help never exists in the daily challenge.

8. Game Center leaderboards and achievements

Game Center is controlled by a switch in the game's Settings, off by default. When off, nothing is connected, sent or queued. When on, the game sends your achievements already earned, then your subsequent scores (see the privacy policy).

  • Daily leaderboard, per language: only your official grid of the day enters it. Its period runs from midnight to midnight in the language's main market: it opens at 05:00 UTC for English and at 23:00 UTC for French and Spanish. A grid submitted outside its draw's period is not sent.
  • Sprint of the week, per language: only your first 3 Sprints of each day count, with or without the Full Edition. The week ends on Monday at midnight in the language's main market.
  • 8 achievements: first grid, streaks of 7, 30 and 100 days, beating the best known grid, playing in 2 then 3 languages, 10 Sprints.

Scores are not checked by a server. Leaderboards have no market value and give no entitlement to any prize.

9. What the Full Edition changes

The Full Edition adds the full archive, unlimited Sprints, detailed statistics and three themes. It never improves a score: same challenge, same rules, same scoring, same leaderboard. It gives neither help in the daily challenge nor a joker.

10. The words

The word lists come from free sources and are cleaned for a game for all audiences: coarse or offensive words are removed. Some may remain, or a word you know may be missing. "Report a word" prepares an e-mail that you choose to send.

A correction arrives through a game update. It never touches a challenge already started or a score already submitted.
