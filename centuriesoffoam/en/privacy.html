Centuries of Foam — privacy policy

Updated 28 October 2026 · Français

1. In short

Centuries of Foam collects no personal data. The game is played without an account, without an Internet connection, without advertising and without any analytics; it relies on no server, neither the publisher's nor anyone else's. Your games, your settings and your unlocked purchases are stored on your device, and the publisher has no access to them.

2. Who is responsible

The game is published by Aplomb (Jérôme Lasalle, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel) trading as Aplomb, 4 impasse du Couchant, 78630 Morainvilliers, France, trade register RCS Versailles 109 075 572), the "publisher". Contact for any question about your data: contact@aplomb-apps.fr.

4. What you share yourself

  • Septmers (the map of the day): when you tap "Copy", the game can copy to the clipboard the code of your score and the journal of your game. The journal contains only the map's date and the orders you gave (no name, no device data). You then share them however you like; the game itself sends nothing. To check a friend's score, the game reads the clipboard only when you tap the button for it; the journal is replayed on your device, and nothing is kept or sent. iOS may ask your permission to paste.
  • Photo mode: the saved picture stays on your device; only you decide whether to share it.
  • Encyclopedia links: the sources cited in the entries open, when you tap them, in your browser, outside the game. The website visited (an encyclopedia, a museum, a library, a public body…) then processes your browsing data under its own policy; the game passes nothing to it.

5. The App Store

The game is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download it or make an in-app purchase, Apple processes the data it needs (your account, the payment, possibly crash reports you agree to share) as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy. The publisher only receives aggregated, anonymous statistics from Apple (downloads, sales, countries) and, if you agreed to it with Apple, crash reports without identity. It never receives your name, your address or your bank details.

6. If you write to us

If you write to the publisher (support, a report), your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you. They are kept three years after the last exchange and then deleted. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in answering its players. Nothing is sold or shared.

7. Children

The game aims at a PEGI 7 rating. It collects no data and has no account, no chat between players and no advertising. In-app purchases go through the App Store, where Apple's parental controls (Ask to Buy, Screen Time restrictions) apply.

8. Your rights

Your saves and settings are on your device: you can erase them by deleting the game. For messages you sent us, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, articles 15 to 21), by writing to contact@aplomb-apps.fr. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

9. Changes

No data will ever be sent by the game without this policy being updated first and without you being told in the game. Any change is dated at the top of this document.
