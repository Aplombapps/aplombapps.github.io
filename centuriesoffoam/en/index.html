Centuries of Foam — support

An island city in three eras · Français

Centuries of Foam is a city builder: in 1480, on an archipelago missing from every map, you found a trading post; your town then crosses time into the present day, and on to the year 2500, when the sea has risen — and what you build stays. The demo is free; the full version is a single purchase. No adverts, no subscription, no account. On iPhone and iPad.

Write to us

contact@aplomb-apps.fr — a question, a bug, an idea: we read everything and reply within a fortnight. For a bug, tell us your device and its system version, the game's version (Settings › About), the era, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a connection?
No. The whole game plays offline, Septmers' map of the day included.

What is free?
The demo: the 1480 era, the first two chapters of the Saga and the first scenario, with no time limit.

What does the full version unlock?
Everything else, in a single purchase: the crossing into the present day and then into the year 2500, the fifteen chapters of the Saga, the twelve scenarios, Septmers, Free Play in all three eras and the creative mode.

And the packs?
Optional: extra decorations and ships, never needed to finish the game. They are bought in the shop (the bag icon during a game), one by one or all together.

I changed device: how do I get my purchases back?
"Restore my purchases", on the home screen or in the shop, with the same Apple account. Nothing is charged.

Does my game move from one device to another?
No: your games are saved on the device, and nowhere else. Your purchases are restored on every device of your Apple account.

Can everyone play?
Adjustable text size, light theme, reduced motion, "How to play" help and the First Steps tour. The VoiceOver screen reader is not supported yet.

My data?
Nothing leaves your device: the game collects no data. The details are in the privacy policy.
