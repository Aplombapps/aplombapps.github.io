3. What is free

With no time limit and no ads: stages 1 to 3 of the Tour, the 12 cars, every reinforcement and workshop upgrade, every game mode, playing together, the Game Center leaderboards and achievements (article 5a), and the four decoration packs, which can also be earned by playing (article 4). 5a. Playing together, and Game Center

Playing together, no account. Playing together at home (split screen, phones-as-controllers, The Pit) goes through the home Wi-Fi, device to device. Playing online with a code (two-player room, The Pit at a distance, Duel between friends, in a derby as in a race) goes through the publisher's online service without creating an account and without keeping any game data (for the IP address, see the privacy policy). Each player appears under a nickname picked from the game's list; no free text, no chat: ready-made messages only. The Duel between friends keeps no rating and does not look for opponents: you play with the person you gave the code to. It earns no Bolts and no reward; on iPhone, iPad and Mac, a win may count on the Game Center "Duels won" leaderboard.

Game Center (iPhone, iPad and Mac). The game has no online leaderboard of its own: it uses Game Center, Apple's gaming service.

  • An Apple service. Game Center is provided by Apple, under its own terms and its own privacy policy. The Game Center account, nickname, friends, age and privacy settings are those of your Apple Account; the publisher creates no account and receives no personal data from Game Center, apart from the leaderboard check described below (see the privacy policy).
  • Optional and discreet. The game asks you nothing at launch: if you are already signed in to Game Center on the device, Apple shows its welcome banner; otherwise, Apple's sign-in window opens only if you press "Sign in to Game Center" (Settings › Data). Without Game Center, the game is the same: nothing is lost, nothing is locked.
  • What the game records there. Your scores on six leaderboards — the Set-up of the day, the Stunt licence of the day, the Show of the day, the time trial of each place, the Parade and Duels won — and about forty achievements earned by playing. No achievement depends on a purchase or asks you to come back every day; only the time-trial leaderboards of the stage pack's places require it (article 4.2).
  • Scores not verified. No server of the publisher checks scores: each device sends its own result to Apple, and cheating remains possible. Leaderboards and achievements have no market value and give no prize and no advantage in the game.
  • Android and Windows. No online leaderboard: the game shows your personal record, kept on the device.

Expected behaviour. No cheating in what involves other players (duels, Game Center leaderboards): not modifying the game or its files, not automating races, not sending false scores, not exploiting a bug on purpose, not disrupting a room or overloading the service.

Reporting and measures. Any player may report abuse or a suspicious score to contact@aplomb-apps.fr (Game Center nickname, leaderboard, date); the publisher answers within 30 days. An inappropriate Game Center profile can also be reported to Apple, from Game Center. Where cheating is established, the publisher may, with the tools Apple provides, remove a score from one of the game's leaderboards and, for repeated or organised cheating, exclude the player from all the game's leaderboards; the rest of the game remains whole. The measure is proportionate. As the publisher has no way of reaching you, it cannot warn you: if you notice a removal, write within 30 days; the publisher gives you the reason, reviews the measure and answers within 30 days, after which you keep the remedies of article 9.

Availability. Online play by code is free and provided with no guarantee of availability: it may be temporarily full or interrupted, in particular for maintenance. Should the publisher stop offering it, players would be told at least three months in advance, and offline play would remain whole. Game Center depends on Apple, which may change, interrupt or withdraw it; the game, your personal records and your progress do not depend on it.

Personal data. What is sent, to whom, and for how long is set out in the privacy policy.

6. Health and safety

  • Photosensitivity. The game contains lightning, fireworks and floodlights. If you or anyone in your family has ever had an epileptic seizure, consult a doctor before playing; stop at once in case of dizziness, blurred vision or muscle twitching. Intense effects can be toned down in the settings.
  • Take regular breaks. In tilt mode, play seated, away from any obstacle.
  • Full Reverse is a game: the driving it shows must never be copied on the road.
