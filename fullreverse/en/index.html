Full Reverse — support

Full reverse ahead! · Français

Full Reverse is a 3D demolition derby: the last car still moving wins. Nobody gets hurt, only metal gets crumpled. Stages 1 to 3, the twelve cars and all the modes are free, with no ads. On iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Write to us

contact@aplomb-apps.fr — a question, a bug, an idea: we read everything and answer everyone. For a bug, tell us your device and its system version, the event or mode you were playing, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or a connection?
No account. Single player, split screen and phones-as-controllers at home work without the Internet. Only the two-player room, The Pit at a distance and the Duel between friends, opened with a code, need a connection.

What is free and what is paid?
Free: stages 1 to 3, the twelve cars, all the reinforcements and all the modes. Paid, optional: the “Stages 4 to 6” pack (the Harbour, the Quarry, the Frozen Lake), a one-off purchase. It brings no car, no reinforcement and no Bolts. Also paid, optional: four decoration packs, shown before you buy, which can also be earned by playing. Refunds are requested from Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed device: how do I get my purchases back?
Tap “Restore my purchases” in the game’s shop, with the same Apple Account. You are not charged. Your progress stays on the device: it only follows through your system backups.

How do I play with others?
At home, up to four on the Mac and two on iPad; phones become controllers over the home Wi-Fi. Online, for two, one player opens a room and gives the other its code.

Is Game Center required?
No. It is used for leaderboards and achievements; without it, the game is the same. To sign in: Settings › Data › “Sign in to Game Center”.

How do I erase my data?
Settings › Data › “Erase all my data” erases what the game keeps on the device, with no way back. Your purchases stay with your store account. Your Game Center scores stay with Apple; files you saved yourself on a computer are not touched. The details are in the privacy policy.
