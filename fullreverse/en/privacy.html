Full Reverse — privacy policy

Version 1.5 of 23 November 2026 · The French text prevails · Français

In short

  • All of Full Reverse can be played without an account. Single player, split screen and phones-as-controllers at home send nothing to the publisher.
  • Playing online with a friend by code (two-player room, The Pit, Duel between friends) needs no account: your game inputs and a few ready-made messages pass through a server; the game keeps nothing there, but the host's logs keep your IP address for 1 day.
  • Game Center (iPhone, iPad, Mac) is optional: it is an Apple service, under Apple's privacy policy. Your scores and achievements go to Apple, not to the publisher.
  • Purchases (decoration packs, "Stages 4 to 6" pack) are paid to the store; the publisher receives neither your identity nor your payment details.
  • "Erase all my data" (Settings › Data) erases in one gesture everything the game keeps about you on the device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers. No data is sold or shared.

These files contain no name, address, advertising identifier or location. The publisher has no access to them.

Files you save yourself on a computer. A ghost challenge saved as a file goes to Documents › Full Reverse (Downloads › Full Reverse on a Mac); a photo, to Pictures › Full Reverse. They are yours and stay where you put them.

System backups. Like any app, these files may be included in your device backups (iCloud, Google, computer backup) depending on your system settings; those backups are Apple's, Google's or yours, not the publisher's.

Sensors. If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the accelerometer to steer the car, frame by frame; nothing is stored. Vibrations can be adjusted or turned off. On iPhone, the system asks you once for local network access, only for phones-as-controllers and The Pit at home.

Erasing your data. Settings › Data › "Erase all my data" erases, after confirmation, everything the game keeps on the device (progress, settings, ghosts, session, photos on the phone): the game starts again as on its first day. Files you saved yourself on a computer (above) are not touched: delete them like any file. Your purchases stay with your store account: "Restore my purchases" brings them back. Your Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple, not by the game: this command does not touch them (section 4). Deleting the app also erases everything on the device.

2. In-app purchases

Payment for the decoration packs and the "Stages 4 to 6" pack is handled only by the store (at release: Apple's App Store; later, Google Play), under its own privacy policy. The publisher receives neither your payment details nor your identity; on the device, the game receives from the store the list of purchases your account owns, to unlock and restore them; it sends it to no one. When a host lends their places in a game played together, the chosen place goes with the game (section 3); no list of purchases is sent. The store gives the publisher aggregated sales reports (number of sales, countries, amounts), with no data about the buyer, which the publisher keeps for its accounts.

3. Playing together, without an account

At home. Split screen communicates with nothing. Phones-as-controllers and The Pit at home go through your home Wi-Fi, device to device, with no server.

Online, with a code (two-player room, The Pit at a distance, Duel between friends). Both devices meet on a Supabase Realtime channel (host described in section 5), named after the room's 6-character code. What passes through: your driving inputs, the chosen car and its paint, the number of your nickname, your licence level, the aids used, and ready-made messages ("Well played!", "Rematch?"…). Your Game Center nickname does not go through it. The game keeps nothing: these messages only pass from one device to the other. As with any Internet connection, the service sees your device's IP address. The host's technical logs record it when the connection opens and keep it for 1 day, for the security and proper operation of the service. The publisher does not use it to identify or track you. This mode provides the game you ask for; it creates no account, keeps no rating and looks for no opponent.

Ghosts. A time-trial challenge is a text (or a file on a computer) containing the place, the car, the time, the run's inputs, the aids and the number of your nickname. You copy it and send it to whoever you like (Messages, e-mail…); it goes through no server of the publisher.

Legal basis. Performance of the contract (article 6.1.b GDPR): sending your inputs is necessary to play with the person you chose, and the game keeps none of it. For the host's technical logs (IP address, 1 day): legitimate interest (article 6.1.f GDPR) in the security and proper operation of the service.

4. Game Center (iPhone, iPad and Mac)

An Apple service. Full Reverse has no leaderboard server: on iPhone, iPad and Mac, it uses Game Center, provided by Apple. Your Game Center account, nickname, friends, age and privacy settings are kept by Apple, under its privacy policy ("Game Center & Privacy", in the Game Center settings and at apple.com/legal/privacy). On Android and Windows there is no Game Center: the game has no online leaderboard and shows your personal record, kept on the device.

Optional, and nothing is asked at launch. At launch, the game only asks the system whether you are already signed in to Game Center; if so, Apple shows its welcome banner. Apple's sign-in window opens only if you press "Sign in to Game Center" (Settings › Data). Without Game Center, the game is the same.

What the game sends to Game Center, once you are signed in: your scores on six leaderboards (the Set-up of the day, the Stunt licence of the day, the Show of the day, the time trial of each place, the Parade, Duels won) and the achievements you earn by playing (about forty). They go straight from your device to Apple, without passing through the publisher. Under Apple's rules, your nickname, avatar and leaderboard scores are visible to other Game Center players; your Game Center settings decide the rest.

What the game reads on the device: whether you are signed in, and your Game Center nickname, to write it in Settings › Data. It does not store it and sends it to no one. The game does not read your friends list, your real name or your address, and does not ask your age.

What the publisher receives: no personal data. Neither your account, nor your nickname, nor your scores are sent to a server of the publisher. The only, occasional, exception: Apple's developer tool (App Store Connect) shows the publisher the top scores of the game's leaderboards, with the Game Center nickname, and lets it remove a score or exclude a player in case of cheating (terms of use, article 5a). The publisher uses it only for that, on a report or for a plainly impossible score, makes no copy and never tries to link a nickname to a person. Legal basis of this check: legitimate interest (article 6.1.f GDPR) in fair leaderboards for all players.

Scores not verified. No server replays your races: a score is not checked before it appears.

To sign out or erase: your device's Game Center settings (Settings › Game Center on iPhone and iPad, System Settings › Game Center on Mac); for your data held by Apple, your Apple Account's privacy page. "Erase all my data" in the game does not touch Game Center.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

  • Data on the device (section 1): under your sole control; "Erase all my data" or deleting the app erases it.
  • Play by code (section 3): the game keeps nothing. Only the host's logs keep your IP address, for 1 day, after which it is erased automatically. For any request, write to the publisher.
  • Game Center (section 4): your data is held by Apple, which is responsible for it; you exercise your rights with Apple. To have one of your scores removed from a Full Reverse leaderboard, or to challenge a removal, write to the publisher giving your Game Center nickname; you may also object to the check described in section 4.

For any question: contact@aplomb-apps.fr; answer within one month.

7. Contact

If you write to contact@aplomb-apps.fr, your address and message are used only to answer you; they are kept three years after the last exchange, five years for a complaint about a purchase. You may also contact the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr) or the data protection authority of your country.

8. Children

The game targets a PEGI 7 rating. The whole game can be played without an account, with others too, and the game never asks for an age. Play by code asks for no name: a child appears there only under a nickname picked from a list, with no free text.

Game Center is an Apple service: a child's account, what it shows others and what it can do (multiplayer, friends) are governed by Apple's settings and by the Screen Time settings parents set on the device. Parents: if you do not want your child to use Game Center, set it in Screen Time; Full Reverse remains whole without it.

A child's purchases go through the store's parental controls (for instance Apple's "Ask to Buy").

9. Security

Exchanges for play by code go through encrypted connections (wss://); those of Game Center, through Apple's connections. The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics module.

10. Changes

Any change to this policy is dated, announced in the release notes and shown in the game before it applies. The publisher keeps the successive versions.
