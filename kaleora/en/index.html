KALEORA — support

All the world's puzzles in a single parlour, for ages three to a hundred and three. · Français

Kaleora brings together jigsaw puzzles and twenty other games — sudoku, nonogram, kakuro, tangram, cubes, sokoban, logic puzzles… — with measured difficulty and hints that explain. Free, no ads, no account. On iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows.

Write to us

contact@aplomb-apps.fr — a question, a bug, an idea: we read everything and answer everyone. For a bug, tell us the game, the level, your device and its version of iOS, Android or Windows, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a connection?
No. Kaleora works entirely offline, daily challenge included.

How do I go from the Workshop to the Parlour?
Through the Workshop's exit, guarded by a parental gate: a two-digit multiplication for an adult to solve. Three mistakes lock it for a minute.

A grid looks impossible.
Every logic grid has a single solution, which can be found without guessing. Ask for a hint: it names the technique that unlocks the grid and shows where to look, without giving the answer first.

I play with a screen reader.
VoiceOver, TalkBack and Narrator read every board cell by cell. The cube, the pyraminx and the tangram are played through the screen reader's actions. Spot the difference and hidden objects, games of the eye, remain out of reach.

How do I erase everything?
Settings › What Kaleora remembers › Erase everything.

My data?
Kaleora collects none: everything stays on your device. The details are in the privacy policy.
