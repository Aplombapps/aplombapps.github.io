The Time Barrier — support

Nine eras. One sky. · Français

The Time Barrier is a vertical shooter through the eras of aviation: a single squadron crosses the Maelstrom, a storm of time that has filled the sky with unmanned machines, from 1909 biplanes to hypersonic gliders. The first three eras are free and complete; eras 4 to 6 and 7 to 9 come in two packs, each bought once. No ads, no energy, no subscription. On iPhone.

Write to us

contact@aplomb-apps.fr — a question, a bug, an idea: we read everything and answer within fifteen days. For a bug, tell us your device and its system version, the game's version (Settings › Legal information), the era and episode, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a connection?
No. The whole solo game, and two players on one screen, play offline. Only remote co-op and Control Tower, the Duel of the skies, challenges sent by code and the leaderboards need the Internet, and only after you agree on the Multiplayer screen.

What is free?
The first three eras in full — thirty episodes, three bosses, three trials —, the ten aircraft and the forty weapons, every mode and multiplayer. Every aircraft, weapon and upgrade is earned by flying.

What is in the era packs?
Each brings three eras of the Adventure: thirty episodes, three piloting trials, three bosses and their Wings. A pack never brings an aircraft, a weapon, an upgrade or currency. The pack of eras 7 to 9 opens after the pack of eras 4 to 6. The offers are on the eras map; payment goes through the App Store.

And the paint shop?
Optional: it sells looks only — liveries, trails, crests, collector aircraft — that never change a flight. You can try everything for free in flight before choosing. Hangar › Paint shop.

I changed devices: how do I get my purchases back?
Settings › "Restore my purchases", with the same Apple Account. Nothing is charged.

Does my progress move from one device to another?
No: it is saved on the device, and nowhere else. Your purchases, though, are restored on every device of your Apple Account.

Can everyone play?
Assists are set one by one (speed, hitbox, shield, aiming, one-hand mode) and never cost a star in the Adventure; text up to 200%, gamepad, reduced effects, colours tested for colour blindness.

My data?
Outside online play, nothing leaves your device. Online, you play under a pseudonym drawn at random; "My data" hands you everything the server keeps about you and "Delete the account" erases it. The details are in the privacy policy.
