Ember to Orbit — privacy policy

Updated 18 October 2026 · The French version prevails · Français

1. In short

Ember to Orbit collects no personal data outside online play. The game is played without an account, without advertising and without any analytics; your games are saved on your device. Only online play, if you agree to it in the game, sends the publisher's server your orders of each turn and a pseudonym drawn at random (section 3 bis); no name, no address, no e-mail.

2. Who is responsible

The game is published by Aplomb (Jérôme Lasalle, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel) trading as Aplomb, 4 impasse du Couchant, 78630 Morainvilliers, France, trade register RCS Versailles 109 075 572), the "publisher". Contact for any question about your data: contact@aplomb-apps.fr.

4. App stores

The game is distributed through stores (Apple's App Store, Google Play, and others where applicable). When you download or buy the game, the store processes the data it needs (your account, the payment, possibly crash reports you agree to share) as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy. The publisher only receives aggregated, anonymous statistics from the store (downloads, sales, countries) and, if you agreed to it with the store, crash reports without identity.

5. If you write to us

If you write to the publisher (support, a report), your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you. They are kept three years after the last exchange and then deleted. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in answering its players. Nothing is sold or shared.

6. Children

The game aims at a PEGI 12 rating. Outside online play it collects no data; online, it asks for none that identifies anyone (a pseudonym drawn at random, no free text: the Relay's handover tips are ready-made phrases).

7. Your rights

For online play, see section 3 bis (everything is done from the game). Otherwise your saves are on your device and you can erase them by deleting the game. For messages you sent us, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), by writing to contact@aplomb-apps.fr. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

8. Changes

No new data will be sent by the game without this policy being updated first and without you being told in the game. Any change is dated at the top of this document.
