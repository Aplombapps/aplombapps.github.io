5. Grids you import, create or share

  • Import. A grid you photograph or type in stays on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); it is never sent to the publisher nor added to the game. Make sure you are entitled to use the grid you import (for instance for your personal use).
  • Shared codes. Ghost duel and Architect produce a text code and a QR code that you alone decide to send, through the iOS share sheet (Messages, AirDrop, Notes…). The code contains the grid and, depending on the case, your game (moves and time) or your rules, as well as the optional first name you write on it (24 characters at most). No code goes through a publisher's server; the publisher neither receives, keeps nor moderates them.
  • Your responsibility. You are the author of what you share and answer for it: do not write in a code a third party's name without their consent, nor anything insulting, defamatory or unlawful, and do not share as your own a grid you have no rights to. The game checks a received code before opening it (complete code, valid grid, unique solution, a game that reaches the solution); this check is technical and does not cover the first name written on it. Only open codes from people you know.
