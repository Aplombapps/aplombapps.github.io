Lucidor — privacy policy

Updated on 22 October 2026. The French version prevails · Français

1. In short

Lucidor collects no personal data. The game plays without an account, without advertising and without audience measurement; it contacts no publisher's server. Your games stay on your device and, if you let it, in your iCloud. The only online services it uses are Apple's: the App Store for the purchase, iCloud for your save, and Game Center if you turn the leaderboards on.

2. Who is responsible

The game is published by Aplomb (Jérôme Lasalle, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel) trading as Aplomb, 4 impasse du Couchant, 78630 Morainvilliers, France, trade register RCS Versailles 109 075 572), the "publisher". Contact for any question about your data: contact@aplomb-apps.fr.

4. iCloud: your save, in your account

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game copies your progress (all of the above except the settings) to your private iCloud database, so you find it on your other devices and after a reinstall. The copy is made at launch, after a won grid and when you turn the switch back on. The publisher has no access to this copy: Apple stores it, in your account, under Apple's privacy policy. You can stop it at any time: game Settings › "Sync with iCloud". To erase the copy already sent: game Settings › "Erase the iCloud copy"; syncing stops and the copy is deleted from your iCloud.

5. Game Center: only if you turn it on

The daily leaderboards are off until you turn them on (game Settings › "Game Center leaderboards"). The game makes no call to Game Center before that. Once on, Apple signs you in to Game Center, then, for each Daily Three grid won on the day, the game sends your time in seconds to that level's leaderboard (Clear, Steady or Expert). Your time and your Game Center nickname are visible to other players. These data are processed by Apple, which is responsible for Game Center, under its own policy; the game neither reads nor keeps any Game Center data.

6. The codes you share

Ghost duel and Architect create a text code and a QR code that you send yourself through the iOS share sheet. The code contains the grid, your game (moves, time) or your rules, and the optional first name you wrote on it: anyone who has the code can read it. Nothing goes through a publisher's server, and the publisher does not receive these codes. A received code is checked, then kept on your device (and in your iCloud copy).

7. Photos, camera and Apple Pencil

  • Importing a grid or reading a QR code: the game asks for camera access the first time you take a photo; for a photo from your library, you pick a single image in the iOS picker, without the game accessing the rest of your photos. The image is read on your device (Apple's Vision recognition); it is neither kept nor sent. Only the grid's recognised digits are kept (section 3).
  • Apple Pencil (iPad): a written digit is recognised on the device; the stroke is neither kept nor sent.
  • The game does not use your location and accesses neither your contacts nor the microphone.

8. The purchase and the App Store

The Complete Edition is bought through the App Store: Apple processes your account and the payment as a separate controller, under its own policy. The game checks on the device, with StoreKit, that the purchase took place; the publisher receives neither your identity nor your bank details. It receives from Apple aggregate, anonymous statistics (downloads, sales, countries) and, if you agreed to it with Apple, crash reports without identity.

9. If you write to us

If you write to the publisher (support, report), your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you. They are kept three years after the last exchange, then deleted. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in answering its players. Nothing is sold or shared.

10. Children

The game is rated 4+ on the App Store. The publisher collects no data, whatever the player's age. Game Center leaderboards and code sharing only work on the player's initiative; Apple's parental controls (Screen Time, Game Center) apply.

11. Your rights

Your progress is on your device and in your iCloud: you erase it by deleting the game and its iCloud copy (section 4). For messages you sent us, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), by writing to contact@aplomb-apps.fr. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

12. Changes

No new data will be sent by the game without this policy being updated first and players being informed in the game. Every change is dated at the top of this document.
